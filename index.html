We’re Still Marching — AR Prototype

This experience uses your camera and plays short audio stories when you scan printed markers.

  1. Tap “Start” and allow camera access when asked.
  2. Hold your phone steady 20–40 cm from each marker until the image appears and audio starts.
  3. Scan in order: Kanji → Hiro → i → Triangle → T.
  4. Move on when the current story ends.

Tip: Headphones improve clarity. If nothing appears, lower your phone slightly or step back.
